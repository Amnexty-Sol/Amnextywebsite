THE IDEA BEHIND AMNEXTY

More traffic doesn't automatically create more business.

A business can have advertising, social media, a website and interested visitors and still lose opportunities. The problem is often not how many people arrive. It is what those people experience after they arrive.

WHERE GROWTH GETS LOST

The gap between attention and action.

Digital growth becomes difficult when individual marketing pieces exist but the customer journey between them is unclear, disconnected or unnecessarily difficult.

01

People arrive but don't act

Traffic reaches the business, but visitors leave without enquiring, booking, buying or taking a meaningful next step.

02

The value isn't immediately clear

Potential customers have to work too hard to understand the offer, who it is for, or why they should choose the business.

03

The journey asks for too much too soon

Visitors are pushed toward a decision before their questions, concerns or level of awareness have been addressed.

04

The journey stops after the first action

A form submission or lead comes in, but weak follow-up means interest cools before the conversation has a chance to develop.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we start a conversation.

A few useful answers about how Amnexty approaches projects.

Do you only build landing pages?

No. A landing page is one possible solution. Depending on the challenge, a project may involve a website, funnel, email journey, automation, conversion strategy or a connected combination of these.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes. Not every project requires starting again. We can examine an existing journey and determine whether specific pages, messaging or follow-up systems should be improved.

What happens before you design anything?

We first understand the audience, business goal, current journey and problem being experienced. That information guides what should actually be designed or built.

Do you work with businesses in every industry?

Our primary experience is concentrated around healthcare and dental, real estate, SaaS and technology, e-commerce, and authors and publishing. Other projects can be considered when the challenge fits our approach.

Can you help if we already generate traffic?

Yes. In fact, existing traffic can make customer-journey problems easier to identify because the question becomes what is happening after people arrive.
